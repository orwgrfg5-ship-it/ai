Windows 11 drawing assistant

Manual Image Drawer

Load an image, drag a box over the screen area you want to draw in, then let the app click the image out manually with darker pixels first.

  1. Open Paint, a browser canvas, or another drawing target.
  2. Load an image here.
  3. Select the draw space box.
  4. Start drawing and do not move the mouse until it finishes.

Controls

Emergency stop: press F8 at any time while the app is running, or click Stop drawing now. The app uses normal mouse input, so keep the target drawing surface focused and do not move the mouse unless you are stopping it.
